# Cheating Accusation Review (SOP)

## Policy Summary
This SOP covers every ticket accusing a player of combat cheating (aimbot, ESP, macros, or similar) under [PvP Rule 2.1](/rules/pvp). It defines what a valid report needs, how evidence is preserved, and how many reviewers must independently agree before staff act.

Punishments themselves live in [Meshing & Cheating Procedures](/staff/cheating). This page governs how an accusation becomes (or fails to become) a confirmed case.

> Never ban, warn, or publicly accuse a player of cheating on a single staff member's read of a clip. Confirmed aimbot/ESP is a permanent ban, so the review standard is deliberately high.
{.is-warning}

## Intake Requirements
A cheating report must include:

- The accused player's in-game name (character names may not be resolvable; gather gamertag or tribe if possible)
- The map/server
- The approximate time of the incident
- A video clip, or a link to one

If no clip is provided, ask once. If no evidence arrives within **48 hours**, close the ticket as unactionable. Log a mod note only if the accused has a pattern of prior reports.

## Preserve Evidence First
Before any review begins, download and archive the clip. Discord CDN links expire and third-party hosts (medal.tv, Streamable) delete freely; a dead link later means the case can never be revisited.

## Review Procedure
1. **Technical first pass**
   - A neutral findings write-up is produced from the clip: hit locations, armor slot damage distribution, snap behavior, and what is observable versus not.
   - This pass produces **no verdict**. It exists so reviewers judge the same facts.
2. **Two independent reviews**
   - **Two staff members with PvP experience**, designated by ownership, each review the clip **independently**: watch before reading the other reviewer's take or any conclusion section.
   - Each records one of three verdicts: **cheating / legit / can't tell**, with a short reason.
3. **Resolve**
   - **Two "cheating"** → actionable. Proceed under [Meshing & Cheating Procedures](/staff/cheating) (confirmed aimbot/ESP is a permanent ban; a discretionary file check may be run first if staff want it).
   - **Split verdict** → a third qualified reviewer breaks the tie.
   - **Two "can't tell"** → no action. Log the ticket as **unproven** with a mod note referencing the archived clip, so a pattern can accumulate across future reports.

> "Unproven" is not "innocent." It means this clip did not carry the case. The clip stays archived and counts as context if the same player is reported again.
{.is-info}

## Reviewer Qualification and Recusal
- Reviewers must have real PvP experience; ownership maintains the list of who qualifies. Ticket staff without it may collect evidence and manage the ticket, but do not cast review verdicts.
- [Conflict of Interest Policy](/staff/conflict-of-interest) applies in full: a reviewer in or near the accused's or reporter's tribe, in an active dispute with either, or subject to credible bias concerns is recused and does not participate in the case discussion.
- Two independent reviewers also satisfies the two-staff rule for significant actions.

## Documentation
The closing record (mod note or ban reason) must include:

- The final verdict and which reviewers cast it
- The archived clip reference
- The ticket link

This is what lets the next accusation against the same name find its history.

## Communicating Outcomes
- The **reporter** is told the outcome in plain terms: action taken, or "reviewed, not proven."
- The **accused** is not contacted on an unproven verdict. There is nothing to answer for, and contacting them only advertises who reported them.
- Never share the findings write-up, reviewer identities' individual verdicts, or archived evidence with players.
- Do not debate verdicts in public channels; the [Conflict of Interest Policy](/staff/conflict-of-interest) script covers favoritism accusations.

## Suggested Staff Response
> Thanks for the report. Cheating accusations are reviewed independently by multiple staff with PvP experience before any action is taken. We'll follow up here once the review is complete. Please upload the clip directly to this ticket if you haven't already.
{.is-info}
