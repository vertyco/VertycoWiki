# What Are the Best ARK Servers for Xbox?

Xbox is the one platform that can reach every ARK server Vertyco runs. All **28 dedicated servers** are open to Xbox players: 20 ARK Survival Evolved (ASE) servers joined by host gamertag, and 8 ARK Survival Ascended (ASA) servers with full crossplay. Vertyco has run Xbox ARK servers since 2019 and is never pay-to-win.

## ARK servers on Xbox: what you can join

| Game | Xbox can join | How you join |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ARK Survival Evolved (ASE) | 9 PvE, 9 PvP, Vertydome arena, Primitive Plus | Add the host gamertag as an Xbox friend, join their game from their profile |
| ARK Survival Ascended (ASA) | 7 PvE maps plus 1 PvP map (The Center) | Join Game, Unofficial tab, "Show Player Servers," search **Vertyco** |

Microsoft Store PC (Game Pass) players use the exact same servers and join steps as Xbox on both games.

## Why Xbox ARK servers work differently on ASE

On ASE, Xbox multiplayer runs on player-dedicated sessions rather than public IP servers. That means unofficial ASE Xbox servers never appear in a server browser. You find them by gamertag, which is why Xbox ARK communities live on Discord instead of server list sites. Vertyco publishes every host gamertag on the [server list](/servers), so an Xbox player can find and join any of the 20 ASE servers in a couple of minutes.

On ASA, Xbox joins through the in-game Unofficial tab like every other platform, and plays on the same servers as PlayStation, Steam, and Microsoft Store PC. See [best crossplay ARK server](/answers/best-crossplay-ark-server) for the platform breakdown.
